Holiday Jalapeño Cranberry Dip

Shared by the Schoettmer Family

Ingredients

Tap to check off ingredients as you shop.

Directions

  1. In a food processor, pulse the cranberries and jalapeño until finely chopped. Strain off any extra cranberry juice.
  2. In a bowl, stir together the cranberry mixture, sugar, lime juice, salt, and cilantro.
  3. In a separate bowl, blend the cream cheese and sour cream until smooth. Spread it onto a festive tray or into a shallow bowl.
  4. Spoon the cranberry jalapeño mixture over the top, garnish with extra cilantro if you like, and serve with crackers.

Cream Cheese Chicken Chili

Shared by the Foreman Family

"Not really a holiday one," the Foremans told us, and it did not even have a name until this book. It is a slow cooker recipe, which makes it a gift on a busy holiday weekend.

Ingredients

Tap to check off ingredients as you shop.

Directions

  1. In a slow cooker, mix the beans, corn, tomatoes, chili powder, onion powder, cumin, and ranch mix until well combined.
  2. Press the chicken down into the mixture and sprinkle the taco seasoning over the chicken.
  3. Add the cream cheese beside the chicken, not on top of it. Cutting the block in half makes this easier.
  4. Cook on low for 6 to 7 hours, until the chicken falls apart easily with a fork.
  5. Move the chicken to a bowl, shred it, and return it to the slow cooker. Stir until the cream cheese is fully melted and combined.

Make it a dip: shred the chicken extra fine and serve it warm with chips.

Cheesy Hash Brown Casserole

Shared by the Williams Family

Ingredients

Tap to check off ingredients as you shop.

Directions

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F and lightly grease a 9x13 baking dish.
  2. In a large bowl, stir together the soup, sour cream, 1/2 cup melted butter, 1 1/2 cups of the cheddar, onion, garlic powder, salt, and pepper until smooth.
  3. Fold in the hash browns until they are evenly coated, then spread the mixture into the dish.
  4. Sprinkle the remaining 1/2 cup of cheddar over the top.
  5. Toss the crushed cornflakes with the 3 tbsp melted butter and scatter them over the casserole.
  6. Bake uncovered for 45 to 50 minutes, until bubbly and golden brown.
Bonus · From Your Gift Bag

Your Holiday Simmer Pot

  1. Empty your kit into a medium saucepan and cover with a few inches of water.
  2. Bring it to a gentle boil, then turn the heat down to the lowest simmer.
  3. Add water every 30 minutes or so to keep everything covered.
  4. Turn it off when you leave the room. Cooled contents keep in the fridge for a day or two of reuse.

Never leave a simmer pot unattended on the stove, and keep it out of reach of kids and pets. The contents are for scent only, not for eating.
